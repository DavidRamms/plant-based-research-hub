Yuan F, Wang L, Nguyen SM et al. — Am J Clin Nutr , 2026 — PMID 41391639

WHAT THIS STUDY CLAIMS

This study found that plant-based diets were associated with a higher risk of certain cancers, including colorectal, liver, and pancreatic cancers, compared to non-plant-based diets.

LIMITATIONS

Observational design, self-reported dietary data, healthy user bias, limited generalisability

WHAT THE BROADER EVIDENCE SAYS

The finding of higher cancer risk with plant-based diets is contradicted by studies showing protective effects of plant-based diets on cancer risk, such as the systematic review and meta-analysis by PMID: 39408238, which found that plant-based diets were associated with improved glycemic control and reduced risk of type 2 diabetes, a known risk factor for certain cancers.

Contradicted by:
