WHAT THIS STUDY CLAIMS

This study found no significant health-promoting benefits of potato protein-based vegan burgers on the intestinal microbiome.

LIMITATIONS

WHAT THE BROADER EVIDENCE SAYS

Soh BXP, Vignes M, Smith NW et al. — Sci Rep , 2026 — PMID 42000958

WHAT THIS STUDY CLAIMS

This study found that plant foods generally supply lower quantities of digestible indispensable amino acids relative to the metabolic requirements, which may be detrimental to muscle mass.

LIMITATIONS

Observational design, self-reported dietary data, limited generalisability

WHAT THE BROADER EVIDENCE SAYS

Contradicted by:

  • The Impact of Whey and Soy Protein Supplementation on Resistance Training in Young Adults: A Systematic Review and Meta-Analysis.
