⚠ Negative Finding Cohort 2026 Protein Adequacy & Muscle Mass

Development of the Vegan Protein Quality (VPQ) tool to derive smarter vegan meals with high protein quality.

Soh BXP, Vignes M, Smith NW et al. — Sci Rep , 2026 — PMID 42000958

WHAT THIS STUDY CLAIMS

This study found that plant foods generally supply lower quantities of digestible indispensable amino acids relative to the metabolic requirements, which may be relevant to protein adequacy and muscle mass.

LIMITATIONS

Observational design, self-reported dietary data, limited generalisability

WHAT THE BROADER EVIDENCE SAYS

The finding that plant-based protein may be lower in indispensable amino acids is contradicted by a meta-analysis (PMID: 41994089) that found no significant difference in muscle strength or body composition between plant-based and animal-based protein supplementation.

Contradicted by:

  • Long-term effects of plant vs. animal protein supplementation on body composition, muscle strength, physical performance, and cardiometabolic risk factors in adults:a systematic review and meta-analysis of randomized controlled trials.
